Our mission

Counterfeit medicines can harm people. We help patients, pharmacists and health workers in Kenya check a medicine quickly, so they can use it with more confidence

Our data

Our verification relies on product records from the Pharmacy and Poisons Board (PPB), the regulator of medicines in Kenya. If a product is not in the records, it could not be confirmed, so ask a pharmacist or contact the PPB.

Know the limits

A result is only as complete as the records behind it. A medicine that is not found is not automatically fake, and a verified medicine still needs to be stored and used correctly. When in doubt, ask a pharmacist.
